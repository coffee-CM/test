## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Thesis
This design system modernizes the institutional presence of Korea's premier golf science and technology university. Moving away from legacy bureaucratic layouts, the UI projects an authoritative, high-performance athletic identity reminiscent of prestigious collegiate athletic programs (NCAA Division I) and high-performance tour organizations (PGA/LPGA), grounded in rigorous academic science and data analytics.

The aesthetic balance merges:
- **Academic Authority**: Grounded, institutional trust powered by deep forest greens, crisp editorial grids, and balanced structural layouts.
- **Athletic Dynamism**: Kinetic visual momentum, bold typography, wide structural framing, and high-impact visual hierarchy.
- **Scientific Precision**: Modern metrics display, clean micro-line structures, and sharp data-driven badges that emphasize biomechanics, data golf, and agronomy technology.

### Visual Style Movement
The design system operates on **High-Contrast Modern Editorial with Kinetic Athletic Accents**. It relies on crisp stark backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), deep authoritative anchor tones (`#064E3B`), and precise tactical neon accents (`#10B981` / `#84CC16`). Surfaces use architectural discipline—crisp divider lines, deliberate structural cards, and subtle frosted overlays for floating navigation and quick-access utility trays.

## Colors

### Strategic Palette Application
The palette creates an immediate psychological connection to pristine fairway turf, master-level academic heritage, and cutting-edge digital tracking hardware.

- **Primary (`#064E3B`) — Deep Masters Forest**: The bedrock of the institution. Used for header banners, anchor cards, primary buttons, high-level headers, and deep institutional brand framing.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`) — Performance Emerald**: Provides energetic contrast against dark surfaces and draws focus to primary conversion paths such as Admissions Apply Now (`원서접수`), active event indicators, and interactive links.
- **Tertiary (`#84CC16`) — Tour Neon Lime**: High-visibility athletic accent used sparingly for micro-status indicators, live tag badges, key statistical metrics, and focus states.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A` / `#042F2E`) — Deep Slate & Forest Obsidian**: High-contrast body typography, dark section footers, and modal overlays.
- **Neutral Light (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) — Crisp Canvas**: Clean, airy background environments that keep complex academic data scannable and free of visual clutter.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Surface Muted**: `#F8FAFC` for alternate content zebra striping and secondary card fills.
- **Border Default**: `#E2E8F0` for subtle hairline division across academic notices.
- **Border Accent**: `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12)` for primary thematic framing.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines & Metric Numbers (Montserrat)**: Imparts a sharp, bold, geometric presence. Display levels utilize heavy weights (700/800) with tightened tracking for impactful, tour-caliber headlines, department callouts, and academic titles.
- **Body & Data Content (Inter)**: Provides pristine legibility for bilingual academic documents, detailed admission guidelines, schedules, and bulletin notices. Features high x-height and clear distinction between glyphs.

### Multilingual Cohesion
When rendering Korean script, the system seamlessly applies system-level Sans (such as Apple SD Gothic Neo or Pretendard) matched mathematically in weight and baseline to Inter and Montserrat, avoiding visual weight discrepancies across English acronyms (PGA, KPGA, KLPGA, KGTech) and Korean titles.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Structure
The system utilizes a **12-column fluid grid system with controlled maximum content widths**:
- **Max Container Width**: 1360px for core editorial academic content; full-bleed with margins for dynamic athletic hero sections and visual galleries.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, 40px outer canvas margin.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer canvas margin.

### Rhythm & Vertical Cadence
- **Section Separation**: Standardized to `space-3xl` (96px) on desktop to provide generous negative space, transforming crowded legacy portals into premium, uncluttered university showcases.
- **Component Clustering**: Card grids and related informational modules use `space-lg` (24px) gaps to maintain distinct structural separation.
- **Quick Links Tray**: A floating or docked horizontal strip crossing the lower bounds of the hero section, offering immediate utility navigation (Registration, Certification, Library, LMS) without cluttering the primary header.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Depth Philosophy
The system combines crisp borders with targeted deep ambient shadows and luminous edge glazes:

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: Clean, pure background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with hair-thin 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Action Tier)**: Low-elevation academic items, notice boards, and department cards. Subtle ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Quicklink Modals)**: Lifted cards and action bars. Expanded ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(4, 47, 46, 0.12), 0 4px 10px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Menus & Quick Trays)**: High-translucency glassmorphic layer: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` and a cast shadow of `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.14)`.
- **Hero Popups & Important Banners**: Inverted dark containers (`#042F2E`) with a soft perimeter glow of `0 0 30px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Semantics & Corner Geometry
Following Level 1 roundedness, geometry remains **disciplined, athletic, and architectural**:
- **Default Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: `rounded` (`0.25rem` / 4px). Sharp, crisp, and precise.
- **Cards & Quicklink Tiles**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Gentle softening that maintains structured, technical stability.
- **Modals, Floating Trays, and Full-Width Panels**: `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- **Tour Status Badges & Live Tags**: Pill shapes (`9999px`) are restricted strictly to micro tags (e.g., "수시 2차 모집중", "LIVE", "D-DAY") to introduce focal variation against structured rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Athletic Action (Admissions / Register)**: Solid deep forest background (`#064E3B`), crisp white text, bold Montserrat tracking (`label-lg`), 4px corner radius. On hover, transitions cleanly to Emerald (`#047857`) with subtle vertical lift (-1px).
- **High-Impact Highlight (원서접수 / Apply Now)**: Solid `#10B981` background with bold contrast typography (`#FFFFFF` or deep `#042F2E`), designed to capture primary admissions traffic.
- **Secondary / Academic Outline**: Transparent surface with 1.5px border in `#064E3B`, colored text. Hover triggers a solid `#F0FDF4` tint.
- **Text Links**: Semi-bold Inter with trailing dynamic arrow icon (`→`) that translates 4px horizontally on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips (Notice board filters: "전체", "학사공지", "장학공지")**:
  - *Inactive*: Crisp `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, 4px border radius.
  - *Active*: Solid `#064E3B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight.
- **Status Tags**: Compact pill badges (e.g., "입찰공고", "일반공지") featuring light emerald or forest tints (`#ECFDF5`) with high-contrast text (`#065F46`).

### Cards & News Grid
- **News & Magazine Card**:
  - Image ratio 16:10 with overflow hidden and subtle zoom on hover (scale 1.04).
  - Metadata row with clean date stamps and publication department.
  - Title in `title-md` Montserrat/Inter, restricted to 2-line clamp.
  - 1px border in `#E2E8F0` with smooth transition to an emerald edge ring (`#10B981`) on focus/hover.
- **Department Spotlight Interactive Cards (골프경기지도학부, 골프산업경영 등)**:
  - Dark, high-contrast photographic backing with a subtle forest-tinted gradient veil (`rgba(4, 47, 46, 0.75)`).
  - Prominent white typographic title with accent color underlines in `#84CC16`.

### Quicklinks Utility Tray (Academic Services)
- Translucent white or forest glassmorphic bar featuring minimalist vector icons (수강신청, 증명발급, 학사시스템, 도서관, 대학식당).
- Top border line in subtle emerald tint, vertical micro-dividers separating utility items.
- Smooth upward transition with glowing icon highlight on hover.

### Notice Board & Tabbed Academic Lists
- Replaces dated table layouts with an editorial list style.
- Clean 1px bottom border per row, category pill on the left, high-readability title in center, and muted date stamp right-aligned.
- Subtle `#F8FAFC` row fill highlighting on mouse hover.
